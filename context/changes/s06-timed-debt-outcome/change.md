## Notes

User can abandon a run or reach timeout and see the debt outcome and final cash score.

Debt clarification: 1,000,000 credits.

Use the existing single end-game screen for all three endings: final score at or above 1,000,000 selects the winning background; a lower score selects the failure background; ship destruction selects the destroyed-ship background.

On New Game, show four full-screen illustrated story screens. Clicking anywhere on a screen or its Next button advances to the next one. Follow them with a separate debt popup stating 1,000,000 credits; keep the amount out of the artwork. Closing the popup starts the run and its 30-minute clock.


The single end-game screen also has a button to return to the main menu.
