## Notes

Confirmed requirements: BIG has 3 projectile-hit points, MEDIUM has 2, SMALL has 1; durability persists through restore. A boosted ship collision uses the existing Moolaris control-lock warning. Planet collisions fragment at a deeper physical boundary and use their own visual effect. Star collisions pull asteroids beneath the star and remove them at half the star radius.
